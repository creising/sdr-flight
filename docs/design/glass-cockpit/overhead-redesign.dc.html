3 · Replay & the timeline · interactive: play, speed, click to scrub
T1One continuous timelinePICK
There is no mode switch to hunt for. Live is the playhead pinned to NOW. Drag it left and you're in replay (UI turns violet); tap NOW and it springs back. The activity histogram is the scrubber, so busy periods are easy to find.
T2Flight-strip lanes
Each recorded flight is a bar on the timeline, packed into lanes and coloured by altitude. Tap a bar to replay just that flight, with the camera following it. Best on desktop or in landscape; needs more vertical space than T1.
T324-hour dial
rotate to scrub · matches the stats clock
A thumb-sized dial on mobile: rotating clockwise moves through the day. Reuses the radial hour chart from Stats, which ties the two screens together. Imprecise for fine scrubbing, so pair it with T1 rather than replacing it.
Mode is a colourEntering replay crossfades accents green → violet over 250 ms and adds a faint violet wash to the map. Live contacts fade out and recorded tracks draw in.
The clock is the heroIn replay the look-up card becomes a big clock with relative time ("3 h 28 m ago"). That's what you actually check while scrubbing.
Controls, minimalOne round play/pause button, one speed pill that cycles 1×→5×→20× on tap, window chips 1h/6h/24h that rescale the strip. No separate scrubber bar.
Tracks show past/futureFlown path = dots brightening toward the plane, path still to fly = sparse dim dots. Reads well on a still frame and while scrubbing.

What needs a light variant
Altitude ramp. The dark-theme ramp is too pale on white. Drop lightness to ~0.58 and keep hue, so coral/amber/blue still read as the same bands (amber needs the most darkening).
Map tiles. Use un-filtered OSM tiles in light, filtered in dark (see right). Range rings switch to dark ink at ~20% opacity.
Elevation. Borders become soft shadows; floating controls go white. Mode colours (green/violet) also darken to L≈0.6.
Switching. Follow prefers-color-scheme, plus a manual override. A third "night" option (dim red/amber, like 1a) is worth considering for a dark bedroom.
Building it with your stack
Dark tiles. OSM's standard tiles are bright. Either add filter: invert(1) hue-rotate(180deg) brightness(.75) saturate(.35) to .leaflet-tile-pane (no new dependency), or use CARTO's dark basemap (OSM data, free tier, requires attribution).
Markers. L.divIcon with an inline SVG chevron and a CSS rotate(). History dots are L.circleMarkers; range rings are L.circle in metres, so they scale with zoom.
Bottom sheet. A scroll container with scroll-snap-type: y mandatory and two snap points. Respect env(safe-area-inset-bottom) for the PWA.
Type. Barlow + Barlow Condensed from Google Fonts (~60 KB). Use font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums everywhere numbers update, so values don't jitter.
Motion. Ease marker positions between updates (CSS transition on the icon's transform), crossfade mode colours, and turn it all off under prefers-reduced-motion.
